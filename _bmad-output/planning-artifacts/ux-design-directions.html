Howera Design Directions

Exploration of three UI directions for frontend planning. Direction B is selected in the UX specification.

Direction A: Operational Console
Alternative
Character: dense, command-centric, utilitarian.
Pros: maximum information density for power users.
Tradeoff: higher cognitive load for everyday editors.
Direction B: Editorial Studio
Selected
Character: balanced, guidance-first, writing-focused.
Pros: strong flow from run to edit to export, with calm status clarity.
Tradeoff: slightly less dense than console-style layouts.
Direction C: Immersive Canvas
Alternative
Character: visual-first with broader spatial layout.
Pros: expressive for screenshot-heavy tasks.
Tradeoff: more implementation overhead for v1 frontend.
